# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and researchers compare sourced agent benchmark results.

## Product purpose

Help readers find relevant benchmarks and inspect evaluation evidence.

## Brand personality

Direct, precise, restrained. Preserve the existing Steel visual identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid promotional score claims, decorative animation, and unsupported comparisons.

## Design principles

- Keep scores linked to their sources.
- Explain scope and comparison limits.
- Use the existing dark surfaces, yellow accent, and compact tables.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Use semantic headings, keyboard links, visible focus, and responsive layouts.

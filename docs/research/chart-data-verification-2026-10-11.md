Published architectural totals take precedence over auxiliary tensor totals; MoE active counts do not determine category. Prices use standard uncached rates for the evaluated version/provider/context tier; GPT-5 nano's $0.05/$0.40 blend is $0.1375, Small. Embedding-only APIs have no fabricated zero output tariff. Historical GPT-4o/Claude aliases and original CUA retain uncertainty rather than borrowing later snapshot rates.
